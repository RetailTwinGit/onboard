Architecture

The stack

Database & Auth
Supabase
PostgreSQL 15, EU West Ireland. Row-Level Security enforces brand isolation at the database level. Every query auto-scopes to what the user can see.
Edge Functions
Deno / TypeScript
12 server-side functions at the edge. No cold starts. Direct access to Supabase service role. Deploy with one CLI command.
AI Engine
Claude Sonnet 4.5
Anthropic API for classification (topic, relevance, sentiment) and voice-matched content generation. Best-in-class reasoning for nuanced output.
Frontend
Lovable
AI-assisted frontend builder. We describe the UI in natural language; Lovable builds React components. Backend-first: Lovable fetches, never calculates.
No custom servers. No Docker. No DevOps. Everything runs on managed infrastructure.

Session Memory

The system remembers everything

Every session starts with full context. CLAUDE.md — the project's brain — auto-loads with the complete state: tables, functions, decisions, gotchas, and open tasks.

Session Discipline
Every session ends with:
1. Update status metrics
2. Mark completed tasks
3. Write session summary
4. Commit and push to GitHub
5. Update CLAUDE.md with learnings

No pasting. No explaining. The next session picks up exactly where you left off.
S&T Prioritization
Goldratt's Theory of Constraints

Every session starts with a Strategy & Tactics analysis. Identify the constraint. Only build what advances it.

In practice: we identified "Conversion" as the active constraint (no external users yet), which kept us focused on the user experience rather than infrastructure optimization.

Session 17

The Knowledge Breakthrough

Before Session 17, the AI sounded like you but didn't know what you know. It used the right tone but missed the frameworks, the insights, the experience.

We solved this with two layers of knowledge injection:

Bio → System Prompt
Identity-level context. The AI knows who you are before it starts writing.
Knowledge → User Message
Publications, expertise, frameworks. Injected at generation time. Updatable without prompt regeneration.
Design Decision
Absorbed. Never cited.
An expert writing a LinkedIn post doesn't cite their own publications. They draw on accumulated knowledge and express it naturally.

Citations create distance between the author and the expertise — the opposite of thought leadership.

The AI absorbs your knowledge as background context. It influences tone, specificity, and framing — without attribution.

Decisions

Why it's built this way

▸ Why a dedicated schema?
All tables in a news schema, not public. Complete isolation from other services. Prevents accidental cross-contamination.
▸ Why backend-first?
Business logic in edge functions, not the frontend. Lovable is a replaceable display layer. Logic is reviewable, testable, and shareable across clients.
▸ Why OTP over magic links?
Magic links fail in SPAs — auth guards strip hash tokens before Supabase can process them. OTP codes establish the session directly. Zero redirect issues.
▸ Why tiered retention?
Low-relevance articles expire in 7 days (noise). High-relevance in 30 days. Articles linked to active drafts are protected. Keeps the pool fresh and relevant.
▸ Why absorbed knowledge?
Thought leaders don't cite themselves. The AI reads your past work and lets expertise influence writing naturally — like experience shapes current thinking.
▸ Why S&T prioritization?
Goldratt's constraint theory prevents scope creep. Every session asks: "What advances the constraint?" This kept us shipping instead of polishing.

The Insight

Software is becoming free.

This system turns that expertise into content at zero marginal cost. The moat isn't the software — it's what you know. And nobody else knows what you know.
